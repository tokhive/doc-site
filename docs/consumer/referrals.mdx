# Referrals

Invite people to TokHive with your own link or code. When they deposit, both of you get credits. If they also supply a subscription as a Provider, you earn a cash share of their sales.

All amounts on this page are **current defaults**. Administrators set them and can change them; a change applies only to rewards given after it. The **Referrals** page in the console always shows the rules as they stand.

## Invite someone

1. In the console, open **Referrals**.
2. Copy your **Invite link** or your **Referral code**; each has a copy button.
3. Send it to the person you want to invite.

An invite link opens the registration page with your code already filled in. Someone who has your code can also type it into the optional **Referral code** field when they register. A code that doesn't exist is refused, and they can correct it or clear the field to register without one.

A referral is set once, at registration. It can't be added to an existing account, or changed later.

When the referral campaign is paused, the **Referrals** page says so. People can still register with your link, but no rewards are paid while it is paused.

## Rewards for deposits

Rewards depend on the total the invited person has deposited on chain. Earnings transferred to their balance don't count. Both rewards are paid automatically when the deposit that reaches the total is credited.

| Their total deposits | They get | You get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $10 | $20 in credits | |
| $100 | $50 of their deposits is locked | $50 in credits |

- **Credits** can't be withdrawn and pay for GPT models only, not Claude models. They are spent first.
- **Locked deposits** pay for any model, Claude included, but can't be withdrawn until they are spent. The lock happens automatically when their deposits reach $100, and can't be undone. On **Balances**, the locked amount is shown as **Locked**.
- **Up to 3 rewards for you.** You can be rewarded for up to 3 people. Anyone you invite after that still gets their own $20.
- **Limited in number.** Both rewards come from a limited total across TokHive. When one runs out, the **Referrals** page says none are left at the moment.

If you registered with a referral code, the **Balances** page and the **Deposit** dialog show what your referral does at each deposit total before you send anything. The sign-up bonus for new accounts is separate, and you can get both.

## Rewards for Provider sales

When someone you invited supplies a subscription as a [Provider](../provider/overview.mdx), you earn a share of each sale they make:

- The share is **20% of the platform's commission** on the sale. For Claude models it is **at least 2% of what the buyer paid**, even when the commission is lower.
- It is paid from the platform's side. Your invitee earns their full price, and the buyer pays nothing extra.
- There is no share when the buyer is you or the person you invited.
- Shares are added to your earnings once a day. Unlike credits, earnings are cash: transfer them to your balance to spend them, or to [withdraw them](../provider/faq.mdx#can-i-cash-out).

## Track your referrals

The **Referrals** page shows totals: **Invited**, **Rewarded you**, **Rewards left** and **Earned (USD)**. Under **People You Invited**, it lists everyone who registered with your link or code, newest first, with their email partly hidden:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Registered | They registered and haven't reached the first deposit total yet. |
| Received their bonus | They got their credits. |
| Reward pending | Their deposits reached the total for your reward, but it hasn't been paid yet, for example because the rewards have run out. It is checked again at their next deposit. |
| Rewarded you | Your reward has been paid; the **Your reward (USD)** column shows the amount. |

Every referral reward also appears on the **Bonuses** tab of the ledger on **Balances**, under **Referral Bonuses**: the credits you got as an invitee (**Referral bonus**) or for inviting (**Reward for inviting**), and the deposits a referral locked (**Deposit locked**).
