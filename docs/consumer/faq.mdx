# Consumer FAQ

Questions Consumers ask about pricing, billing, accounts, using TokHive and data. For the endpoints themselves, see the [API reference](../api/overview.mdx).

## Pricing and billing

### How is the price of a request set?

Every model has an official price: the vendor's own API price per million tokens, with separate prices for input, output, cache reads and cache writes. A Provider's price is that official price times the Provider's quote multiplier. You pay the Provider's price plus the platform commission: `official price × multiplier × (1 + commission rate)`.

Different Providers can charge different prices for the same model. A request goes to an available account in good health: one that already holds your conversation, if there is one, and otherwise usually the cheapest. The price is fixed when the request is dispatched, and **Call Records** shows what each call cost.

### When am I charged?

Only for a response that completed and whose [execution receipt](../trust/execution-receipts.mdx) verified, by the tokens it actually used. A request that fails or is interrupted costs nothing, even if it had started to stream.

### How do I add funds?

Deposit USDT0 on Conflux eSpace, currently the only accepted stablecoin; card and bank payments aren't supported yet. Your deposit address is yours permanently, and a deposit is credited in US dollars automatically after the network's confirmations. If your USDT is on another network or an exchange, [Deposits](./deposits.mdx) shows how to bring it to Conflux eSpace.

### Why did a request cost more than the lowest listed price?

The [price list](https://tokhive.ai/models/) shows the lowest price on TokHive right now. A single request can cost more when:

- **Your conversation stays on its account.** A follow-up goes to the account that already holds the conversation, so it keeps its context and prompt cache, even if a cheaper Provider is available.
- **It enters a long-context tier.** Some models charge more once a request's input, cached tokens included, passes a model-specific threshold. The whole request, output included, is then billed at that model's long-context prices.
- **It writes to the prompt cache.** Cache writes have their own price where the model charges for them.
- **It's served by fallback supply.** See [What happens when no Provider can take my request?](#what-happens-when-no-provider-can-take-my-request)

**Call Records** shows each call's tokens and cost.

### Why did my balance go below zero?

A request only needs a balance above zero to start, and it is charged when it finishes. A long request can therefore take the balance below zero. New requests are refused with `402 insufficient_balance` until you add funds.

### Can I withdraw my balance?

Yes, the withdrawable part of it: funds you deposited and earnings you transferred in. Credits granted by TokHive can only be spent on requests, and they are spent first. **Balances** shows how much you can withdraw.

1. In **Account**, set up an authenticator app. Every withdrawal asks for a code from it.
2. On **Balances**, add a receiving address under **Withdrawal Addresses**. A new address can be used 24 hours after you add it.
3. Click **Withdraw**, choose the network, address and amount, and enter your authenticator code.

- The minimum is $10.
- The fee is the network's actual gas cost. It's taken from the amount, and the form shows it before you confirm.
- Withdrawals over $1,000, or beyond a daily limit for automatic approval, are reviewed by hand before they are sent.
- You can't withdraw while you have requests running.

For refunds or other questions about your balance, email [hello@tokhive.ai](mailto:hello@tokhive.ai).

### Can I get an invoice?

TokHive doesn't issue invoices yet. **Call Records** lists every call with its cost, and **Balances** lists your deposits, charges and withdrawals. If you need documents for accounting, email [hello@tokhive.ai](mailto:hello@tokhive.ai).

## Account and keys

### I lost my API key, or it may have leaked

A key is shown only once, when it's created, and TokHive keeps only a hash of it, so it can't be recovered. On the **API Keys** page, revoke the old key, which stops it working right away, and create a new one. You can have several keys, for example one per machine; they share your balance and each runs up to 20 requests at once.

### I forgot my password

If you set up an authenticator app in **Account › Password and Recovery**, click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and reset it with a code from the app. Otherwise, email [hello@tokhive.ai](mailto:hello@tokhive.ai) from the address you signed up with, and we'll send you a one-time reset link.

Changing your password doesn't affect your API keys. Revoke them separately if you think they were exposed.

## Using TokHive

### Which tools work with TokHive?

Any tool that speaks the OpenAI Responses or Chat Completions API, or the Anthropic Messages API. Step-by-step guides cover [Codex](./codex.mdx) and [Claude Code](./claude-code.mdx), and [CC Switch](./cc-switch.mdx) switches them between TokHive and other providers. The console's **Setup guides** also cover OpenCode and the official SDKs. Streaming, tool calls and prompt caching work as in the vendors' own APIs.

Some sampling parameters aren't accepted by the subscription upstream, for example `temperature` and `max_output_tokens` on the Responses API. TokHive drops them and names them in the `X-Hive-Ignored-Params` response header; see [Responses](../api/responses.mdx).

### Why can't my conversation continue?

Some conversations can only continue on the account that served them. If that account becomes unavailable, the request returns `409 session_context_unavailable`. When the response has a `Retry-After` header, retry after that time; otherwise start a new conversation.

## Availability

### Which models can I use?

The ones listed by [`/v1/models` and `/anthropic/v1/models`](../api/models.mdx): enabled, priced, and offered by at least one online Provider. The list follows the Provider network, so a model can disappear while nobody offers it.

### What happens when no Provider can take my request?

The request may be served by a **vetted premium API service** instead, so that it still succeeds:

- It is billed at that service's price, which can be higher than a Provider's.
- Your request and the response pass through that service.
- The execution receipt proves that the request reached that service, not which vendor account served it.

Call Records shows which source served each attempt. This fallback is on by default. To turn it off, open **Account › Fallback Supply**. With it off, such a request returns `503 no_supply` instead.

### Why do I get `503 no_supply`?

No account can serve the model right now, and fallback supply is off or has nothing for the model. When accounts are only busy or briefly rate limited, the `Retry-After` header says when to retry. See [Errors](../api/overview.mdx#errors).

### Is Claude fast mode available?

No. Requests that ask for fast mode run at the standard speed and are billed at the standard price. See [Messages](../api/messages.mdx#fast-mode-is-not-offered).

### How many requests can I run at once?

Up to 20 at a time per API key. More return `429 concurrency_limit`.

## Data and privacy

### Who can see my prompts?

- **Hive**, the marketplace service, handles your prompts and responses in plaintext to route them.
- **The TEE** executes your request against the vendor.
- **Providers** cannot see your prompts, your responses or who you are; their machine only relays encrypted bytes.
- **The upstream vendor** (OpenAI or Anthropic) processes the request as it does any other.

End-to-end encryption that would hide prompts from Hive too is planned. See [Trust Model](../trust/trust-model.mdx).

### What does TokHive keep?

Call records keep metadata only: endpoint, model, timing, status, usage, cost and receipt digests. When a call fails, Hive also keeps your request and the upstream's response in restricted diagnostic logs, to investigate the failure, and deletes them after 3 months. Nothing is kept for successful calls.

### Is my data used to train models?

No. TokHive doesn't use your prompts or responses to train models, and doesn't sell them.

### Can I check that my request really reached OpenAI or Anthropic?

Yes. Every execution has a TEE-signed receipt naming the upstream host, and Hive verifies it before you are charged. You can also verify it yourself: download the receipt from **Call Records** and check it with the open-source verifier at [verifier.tokhive.ai](https://verifier.tokhive.ai/). See [Verify a receipt yourself](../trust/execution-receipts.mdx#verify-a-receipt-yourself).
