# Before You Supply

This page collects what you should know before you decide to supply a subscription: the risk to your account, what you earn, what it does to your own quota, what it can cost you, and how you stay in control.

## Which subscriptions can be supplied

| Platform | Official client | Plans Bee recognizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenAI | Codex CLI | Free, Go, Plus, Pro (including the Pro Lite and Pro Max tiers), Team, Business, Enterprise, Edu |
| Claude | Claude Code | Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise |

A plan takes work only when the marketplace has set a concurrency limit for it. Otherwise the account shows `plan_not_configured`. Bee reads the plan from the official client's login. You can't choose it.

:::caution[Workspace accounts]
A Team, Business, Enterprise or Edu seat belongs to the organization that pays for it. Supplying it may break that organization's own rules as well as the vendor's terms. Workspace administrators can usually see usage, and on some plans also content, so work you supply may show up in the organization's usage or compliance records. Supply a workspace seat only if you are entitled to.
:::

## Account risk

OpenAI's and Anthropic's consumer terms don't allow an account to be shared with others. A vendor may restrict or suspend an account it considers shared. **TokHive offers no compensation if that happens to an account you supply.**

TokHive is designed to keep that likelihood as low as it can, by making supplied work look and behave like your own use of the official client:

- **Your network.** Every upstream connection leaves from your own IP and your usual network, not from a shared server.
- **The official client's identity.** Requests are sent in the official client's own format, with its headers and a current release. For OpenAI, that is the Codex version installed on your machine.
- **Your plan's pace.** An account never runs more requests at once than the concurrency limit set for its plan.
- **Stopping at limits.** Bee stops at a fully used quota window. When the upstream rate-limits the account, it stays out of rotation until the time the upstream gives.
- **One account per conversation.** A conversation stays on the account that started it, rather than jumping between accounts.
- **Your login untouched.** Bee doesn't sign in, sign out or refresh tokens unless you turn on Claude token refresh. Bee also doesn't switch accounts behind your back: a change of account waits for your confirmation.

**Consumer content is not reviewed today.** Consumers' requests run on your account, and TokHive does not yet check what they ask. The vendor's own safety systems still apply to every request, as they do to your own use. A check that refuses requests for illegal content is planned.

Weigh the risk against the return. For an account that supplies regularly, earnings are expected to cover the subscription fee quickly.

## What you earn

- **Your price.** Every model has an official price: the vendor's own API price per million tokens, with separate prices for input, output and cached tokens. Your price is that official price times your **quote multiplier**. The platform default is currently **0.1**, that is 10% of the official price. You can keep it, or set your own multiplier, between 0 and 10, on the **My Quote** page. Your multiplier applies to all models, and the page previews the resulting price of each one.

  For example, at the default 0.1, a model whose official prices are $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens pays you $0.50 per million input tokens and $2.50 per million output tokens.
- **The commission is not taken from you.** The platform adds its commission on top of your price when it charges the Consumer. You receive your full price.
- **When several accounts can serve a request, the cheaper one usually wins.** Accounts in good health come first, and a conversation stays on the account that started it. Among the rest, the lowest multiplier is picked first. A lower multiplier means more work, and a higher one means more per token.
- **You are paid per verified execution.** An execution is settled only when it completed and its [execution receipt](../trust/execution-receipts.mdx) verified. Failed executions earn nothing and cost the Consumer nothing. Rarely, one fails after the upstream has already done some of the work, which uses a little of your quota unpaid. Each settled execution appears in the **Earnings** ledger on the **Balances and Ledger** page.
- **Spending your earnings.** On the **Balances and Ledger** page, **Transfer earnings** moves them to your buyer balance, where they pay for your own API calls. The transfer is one-way.
- **Cashing out.** Withdrawing earnings to a stablecoin wallet is coming soon. Once it is available, a withdrawal arrives in about 5 minutes. The [Terms of Service](https://tokhive.ai/terms) set out the withdrawal rules.

## Your own quota

- Supplied work counts against the **same subscription quota** you use yourself. No part of it is reserved for you.
- Bee keeps taking work until any of your quota windows (for example the five-hour or weekly window) is **100% used**. Then the account shows `quota_exhausted`, and supply resumes after the window resets.
- Supplied work is not added to your ChatGPT or Claude conversation history. OpenAI requests are sent with `store: false`, and Claude requests go through Claude Code's API, not claude.ai. It does show in the usage your vendor reports for the account.
- To keep quota for yourself, pause supply while you work: `bee pause --platform openai` (or `claude`), then `bee resume` when you are done. Work already running finishes; no new work starts.
- Each account runs at most a fixed number of requests at once, a limit the marketplace sets per plan. When it is reached, new requests wait or go to other providers (`concurrency_full`).

## Usage that is billed beyond your subscription

Your subscription is prepaid, but both vendors can also charge for usage beyond it:

- **OpenAI:** a ChatGPT account can hold paid **credits**, which can be spent once the plan's limits are reached.
- **Claude:** a subscription can have **extra usage** turned on, a prepaid balance billed at API prices once the plan's limits are reached.

Bee stops taking work when a quota window is fully used. Requests that are already running near that boundary can still spill over into paid usage. **If you don't want to risk paying anything beyond your subscription, turn off extra usage (Claude) and keep no paid credits (OpenAI) on the account you supply.**

Claude's **fast mode** is billed to extra usage from the first token. TokHive never sends a fast-mode request to your account: it runs every Claude request at the standard speed.

## What runs through your machine

- **Every upstream connection leaves from your own IP**, as it would when you use Codex or Claude Code yourself. Bee connects only to your Hive and to `chatgpt.com:443` or `api.anthropic.com:443`.
- **Bee relays encrypted bytes** and can't read them. The prompts and responses of Consumers are not visible to you or to anything else on your machine. See [Trust Model](../trust/trust-model.mdx).
- **Network use** is the size of the Consumers' requests and responses. Requests from coding agents carry the whole conversation and are often several hundred kilobytes each, so expect noticeable upload traffic while you supply. CPU and memory use are small.
- The machine has to be awake and online while you want to supply.

## Your login

- Bee only reads the official client's login. Your access token leaves your machine only sealed to the TEE, and your refresh token stays in the official client's store. The one exception is Claude token refresh, which is off by default.
- Hive can't open the sealed token. The TEE can use it only through a connection that your running Bee relays: **when Bee is stopped, no request can run on your account.**
- You can keep using Codex or Claude Code on the same account while Bee supplies it.

## Staying in control, and leaving

| You want to | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Pause one platform | `bee pause --platform PLATFORM`; `bee resume --platform PLATFORM` to continue. |
| Stop supplying for now | Stop Bee. Hive stops sending work immediately, and there is no penalty. |
| Stop supplying for good | Stop Bee and [uninstall it](./install.mdx#uninstall). Without a running Bee, the token it sealed can't be used, and it lapses when the access token expires. |
| Supply a different account | Sign in to it in the official client and confirm the change with `bee confirm-account`. See [I switched accounts](./troubleshooting.mdx#i-switched-accounts-in-the-official-client). |

The pairing itself stays on your TokHive account after you stop. You don't need to remove it, and a paired Bee that is not running takes no work. To delete your TokHive account and its data, email [hello@tokhive.ai](mailto:hello@tokhive.ai).

## Suspension by the marketplace

The marketplace administrator can suspend supply from a provider, or from one of its platforms. A suspended account shows `banned` in `bee status` and on the **Bee Device** page, and takes no work until the suspension is lifted. Work already running finishes and is paid. Your earnings are not frozen: you can still transfer and spend them, and still buy as a Consumer. To ask about a suspension, email [hello@tokhive.ai](mailto:hello@tokhive.ai).
