# Consumer FAQ

Questions Consumers ask about pricing, billing, availability and data. For the endpoints themselves, see the [API reference](./api/overview.mdx).

## Pricing and billing

### How is the price of a request set?

Every model has an official price: the vendor's own API price per million tokens, with separate prices for input, output, cache reads and cache writes. A Provider's price is that official price times the Provider's quote multiplier. You pay the Provider's price plus the platform commission: `official price × multiplier × (1 + commission rate)`.

Different Providers can charge different prices for the same model. A request goes to an available account in good health: one that already holds your conversation, if there is one, and otherwise usually the cheapest. The price is fixed when the request is dispatched, and **Call Records** shows what each call cost.

### When am I charged?

Only for a response that completed and whose [execution receipt](./trust/execution-receipts.mdx) verified, by the tokens it actually used. A request that fails or is interrupted costs nothing, even if it had started to stream.

### How do I add funds?

Deposit a stablecoin: USDT0 on Conflux eSpace, or USDT on Ethereum. Your deposit address is yours permanently, and a deposit is credited in US dollars automatically after the network's confirmations. See [Add balance](./quickstart.mdx#3-add-balance).

### Why did my balance go below zero?

A request only needs a balance above zero to start, and it is charged when it finishes. A long request can therefore take the balance below zero. New requests are refused with `402 insufficient_balance` until you add funds.

### Can I withdraw or refund my balance?

Balances can't be withdrawn from the console. For questions about your balance, including refunds, email [hello@tokhive.ai](mailto:hello@tokhive.ai).

## Availability

### Which models can I use?

The ones listed by [`/v1/models` and `/anthropic/v1/models`](./api/models.mdx): enabled, priced, and offered by at least one online Provider. The list follows the Provider network, so a model can disappear while nobody offers it.

### What happens when no Provider can take my request?

The request may be served by a **vetted premium API service** instead, so that it still succeeds:

- It is billed at that service's price, which can be higher than a Provider's.
- Your request and the response pass through that service.
- The execution receipt proves that the request reached that service, not which vendor account served it.

Call Records shows which source served each attempt. This fallback is on by default. To turn it off, open **Account › Fallback Supply**. With it off, such a request returns `503 no_supply` instead.

### Why do I get `503 no_supply`?

No account can serve the model right now, and fallback supply is off or has nothing for the model. When accounts are only busy or briefly rate limited, the `Retry-After` header says when to retry. See [Errors](./api/overview.mdx#errors).

### Is Claude fast mode available?

No. Requests that ask for fast mode run at the standard speed and are billed at the standard price. See [Messages](./api/messages.mdx#fast-mode-is-not-offered).

### How many requests can I run at once?

Up to 20 at a time per API key. More return `429 concurrency_limit`.

## Data and privacy

### Who can see my prompts?

- **Hive**, the marketplace service, handles your prompts and responses in plaintext to route them.
- **The TEE** executes your request against the vendor.
- **Providers** cannot see your prompts, your responses or who you are; their machine only relays encrypted bytes.
- **The upstream vendor** (OpenAI or Anthropic) processes the request as it does any other.

End-to-end encryption that would hide prompts from Hive too is planned. See [Trust Model](./trust/trust-model.mdx).

### What does TokHive keep?

Call records keep metadata only: endpoint, model, timing, status, usage, cost and receipt digests. When a call fails, Hive also keeps your request and the upstream's response in restricted diagnostic logs, to investigate the failure, and deletes them after 3 months. Nothing is kept for successful calls.

### Can I check that my request really reached OpenAI or Anthropic?

Every execution has a TEE-signed receipt naming the upstream host, and Hive verifies it before you are charged. **Call Records** shows the receipt digests. Independent verification by Consumers is part of the planned [Web Proof](./web-proof/web-proof.mdx) work.
