![New account? $10 in GPT credits, added the moment you set up an authenticator app. Limited supply, and $5 more for each confirmed bug report.](/img/blog/signup-bonus-cover.png)

TokHive is new, and we want early users to put it through real work. Create an account, set up an authenticator app, and we add **$10 of credits for GPT models** to your balance. Report a problem we can confirm and you get $5 more. The number of bonuses is limited.

{/* truncate */}

## How to claim it

![Register at console.tokhive.ai/register, set up an authenticator app under Account › Password and Recovery, and $10 of credits for GPT models is added to your balance](/img/blog/signup-bonus-steps.svg)

1. Register at [console.tokhive.ai/register](https://console.tokhive.ai/register).
2. Open **Account**. Under **Password and Recovery**, find **Authenticator app** and click **Set up**. Scan the code with Google Authenticator or a similar app, then enter the 6-digit code.
3. The $10 is added to your balance as soon as setup succeeds.

Then create an API key and make your first call. The [QuickStart](/quickstart) covers any SDK, and [Set Up Codex](/consumer/codex) connects the Codex CLI in about five minutes.

## What the credits cover

- **GPT models only.** Credits don't pay for Claude models. To use Claude, [add funds](/consumer/deposits) with a stablecoin deposit.
- **Not withdrawable.** Credits can only be spent on requests.
- **No expiry.**

Current models and prices are on the [models page](https://tokhive.ai/models).

## The fine print

- One bonus per person. Accounts created before the bonus was introduced don't qualify.
- At most three bonuses per network, such as one home or office connection. If your network has reached that limit, open **Account** from another network and click **Replace** to receive yours.
- When all bonuses have been given out, the offer disappears from the console.
- TokHive decides how these rules apply, including whether a report qualifies. Its decision is final.

## Report a problem, earn more credits

If something breaks or doesn't work as documented, tell us. The first report of each problem we can confirm earns another **$5 of credits**, on the same terms as the sign-up bonus.

**What counts:** requests that fail, hang or are cut off because of a fault on TokHive's side; wrong charges or balances; a supported client or SDK that doesn't work as documented; console errors that stop you from completing a task.

**What doesn't:** network or connectivity problems, including proxies; failures caused on your side, such as client configuration, an invalid key, an empty balance or a malformed request; feature requests; small usability or cosmetic improvements; the quality of a model's answers; documentation errors; and anything the docs describe as expected or unsupported.

Post it in the bug reports channel on [Discord](https://discord.gg/nrVU4vSShw) or in our [Telegram group](https://t.me/tokhive): say what you did, what happened, and the request ID if a call was involved (copy it from the call's detail page in the console). We reply there once we've confirmed it.

To receive the credits, email hello@tokhive.ai from your account's email address with a link to your report. Don't post your account email in public. We never message you first, and never ask for your password, API key or funds.

Found a security problem? Don't post it in public. Report it privately as described on our [Security page](https://tokhive.ai/security).
